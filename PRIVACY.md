_Last updated: 2 September 2026_

## What the app collects

**Voice.** When you tap the microphone, audio is passed to the speech recognition service built
into your device (on most phones, Google's) so it can be turned into text. Voice Search does not
record, store, or upload audio itself. How that recognition service handles the audio is covered
by your device vendor's own privacy policy.

**Searches.** The text you search for is sent to the search engine you selected (Google, YouTube,
Bing or DuckDuckGo) by opening it in your browser. Your most recent 20 searches are stored on the
device so they can be shown to you again. They are not sent to us, and they are excluded from
Google cloud backup; they transfer only if you move directly to a new device.

**Crash reports.** If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics receives a stack trace along with the
device model, OS version, and a random installation identifier. This is used only to fix bugs.

**Ads.** The app shows banner ads through Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID. In the
EEA and the UK you are asked for consent before any ad is requested, and you can change that
choice at any time.

## What the app does not do

There is no account, no login, and no server operated by this app. Nothing you say or search for
is transmitted to the developer.

## Your choices

- Revoke microphone access at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Voice → Permissions.
- Clear stored searches with "Clear all" on the home screen.
- Reset your ads consent choice from the AdMob consent form.
